# Code of conduct

Leftovers is a small project and we want it to be a friendly place to contribute.

**Please**
- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and explain the reason behind it.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

**Not okay**
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or trolling.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including financial details.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people
who keep breaking them.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through [github.com/saisaharsh3](https://github.com/saisaharsh3).
