Know what's left.

Leftovers is an expense tracker for Android that answers one question first: how much can you spend today? Your budget, what you've spent and the bills still to come, worked out on your phone and kept there.

Everything in four tabs and a few taps

  1. Home

    Today's number first, then your balance across accounts, payments it spotted for you, and the bills coming up.

  2. Add

    The keypad opens first. Two taps and it's logged, on today or any day you forgot. Split it with friends or across categories.

  3. Insights

    Touch any day's bar to read it, see where the month is heading, and what changed since last month.

  4. Plan

    Your budget, the subscriptions it already knows, and how much to put aside each month for something you want.

  5. Activity

    Every day's spending on a calendar. Search by note, amount, account, category or #tag.

  6. Money owed

    Who owes you and whom you owe, kept out of your budget, and paid back in full or a bit at a time.
